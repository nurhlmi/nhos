## Checked and not findings (with the reason)

- Lucide icons: kept by the owner (DESIGN.md override). Individual irrelevant icons are listed above (#9, #10).
- Dark default, Inter, sidebar + topbar dashboard shell, "View all" buttons: requested in the owner's brief (DESIGN.md).
- Numbers, deltas, feeds, KEV dates, news, market values: all come from real sources or are labelled estimates; no invented metrics (R-17, R-38). Change indicators name their comparison period.
- Status badges (severity, KEV, asset status, confidence) mark real states (R-09).
- Navigation: every sidebar, command-menu and profile-menu item has a real destination (R-24).
- Empty, loading and error states exist on every data view (R-27); #15 is about wording only.
- Motion: spinners and hover only, matching MOTION 1 (R-19). No emoji, no glow, no glassmorphism beyond the topbar backdrop (R-10, R-13).
- Dark theme text contrast: muted text 6.79:1 on card, 6.25:1 on raised surface; blue links 5.54:1; amber 8.02:1, green 6.69:1, red 4.50:1 on their badges (pass).
- Light theme: muted text 5.68:1, white on primary buttons 4.81:1, red text on white 4.99:1 (pass).
- ATS CV template uses uppercase section headings: that is the CV/ATS convention inside the printed document, not UI decoration.
- Mobile at 390px: no horizontal overflow on the pages screenshotted earlier (overview, assets); #6 covers tap target size.
